## What was wrong, what changed

1. **Facing (root cause, also the seats).** The Player root is rotated: main.tscn spawns it turned 90° (and the start
   screen copies the spawn's basis onto it), but `player.gd` wrote *world* yaws (`atan2(dir)` while walking, the seat's
   `global_rotation.y` when sitting) into the Body's *local* `rotation.y`. Every facing came out 90° off the root's
   yaw. `smoke_doors_sit` checked the local value against the seat yaw, so it agreed with itself and never saw it.
   Fix: `Player.facing()` / `face(yaw)` read and write the Body's *global* yaw; walking turns toward the real motion
   (after sliding along furniture), toward the input while blocked, and keeps its facing while stopping. A second,
   smaller cause: tap-walks overshot their last waypoint and turned the figure round on arrival (fixed below).
2. **Seats.** All 120 seat spots were already right (`test_seats` walks the list: chairs and banquettes face their
   table, counter stools face the bar counter, the tiny two-stool tables face the stage over their table, the memory
   chairs face the room with their backs to the TV corner). The wrong-way sitting was point 1. Sitting now slides and
   turns onto the seat over 0.3 s (`SIT_TIME`) instead of snapping.
3. **Movement.** One acceleration on the whole horizontal velocity (was per axis, which bent diagonal starts and
   stops): `accel` 14 m/s² speeding up / turning, `decel` 22 m/s² stopping (full speed in 0.27 s, stop in 0.18 s);
   `turn_rate` 14/s. Tap-walks: pure pursuit on the navmesh path (steer 0.45 m ahead along it, slow down over the last
   0.9 m, arrive within 4 cm), so corners are one curve and the end is never overshot. Stairs and ramps:
   `floor_snap_length` 0.4, `floor_constant_speed`, `floor_max_angle` 50° (0 bounces on both flights). The capsule stays
   0.35 m: a thinner one let a stick-walker wedge between the tiny-table stools.
4. **Camera** (`camera_rig.gd`). Critically damped springs instead of a first-order lerp: the look point follows in
   `follow_time` 0.3 s with a lead of `lead_time` 0.4 s of the player's velocity (max 1.6 m), so what is ahead is in
   view; yaw / pitch / distance ease on their own spring (`swing_time` 0.6 s); look height follows the player's feet
   (stairs). The main room is now **one framing** (`build_venue.gd` `_camera_zones`: floor, bar, stage and toilets all
   look east with the same pitch, distance and a clamp wide enough for every corner), so the view only turns at a
   threshold: lobby, corridor, Sticker door, lounge, buffer, main room, red door. Zone names are unchanged (HUD title,
   band earshot, arrival bonus). Lounge clamp widened a little for its corners.
5. **Walls.** Entrance corridor (camera zones `lobby`, `corridor`): unchanged rule, pieces just hide, lounge stays solid
   until the Sticker door. Everywhere else a cutaway piece is cut when it crosses the sight line to the player, or is
   in view and wholly nearer to the camera than the player (whatever room it belongs to), or by its `cut_normal`
   inside its own `cut_rooms`. A cut full-height box wall sinks in 0.18 s to a **0.4 m stub** (10 % of the 4 m room;
   a collider-less twin, so physics is untouched); kit pieces on walls and lintels just hide. Kit cutaway pieces are
   now tested with the box of all their meshes (they used a 0.6 m cube at their origin, which is why the buffer
   window panels never cut). The upper west wall of the main room (`UpperW*`, above 4 m) is cutaway instead of a
   sight-line occluder. Result: the main room's NW and SW corner tables, the buffer zone ("meeting room": I read it as
   the buffer zone with its four tall tables, hidden by the old-toilets block and the mirror wall in front of it) and
   the lounge tables are all in view with no standing wall in between.

## Hook for camera effects (drunk sway, shake, close-ins)

`CameraRig.fx_offset` (world metres), `fx_tilt` (extra pitch, yaw, roll in radians), `fx_fov_scale` (multiplier).
Set them every frame you want them; they are added after the smoothing, never clamped, and do not move `rig.yaw`, so
the stick keeps its meaning. Reset to `Vector3.ZERO`, `Vector3.ZERO`, `1.0`. A cut-scene camera (WASTED close-in) can
simply be made current; make `rig.camera` current again and call `rig.snap()` after. The nightlife worker shipped its
drunk effect as a screen overlay and WASTED with its own camera, so nothing uses the hook yet.
