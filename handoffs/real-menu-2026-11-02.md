# Handoff: Black Cabin's real menu in the game (🍸 menu worker, 2026-11-02)

## What landed
- **Transcription of record** `docs/venue/menu/menu.json` (+ readable `menu.md`): all 11 pages, 20 sections,
  154 printed items, the house shooters deal (5 + 1 free, 590), Seppuku and the five bottle bundles with was/now
  prices and the "mood and star position" note. Names copied exactly as printed (case, punctuation, misspellings);
  apostrophes normalised to `'`. Stars, chillies, wine ABV, region and tasting notes, bottle / glass for spirits,
  the two printed Thai names. Every price checked twice: full page, then zoomed crops.
- **`print_errata`** (10 entries; not fixed in the transcription): THAI CAVIER, FRITTES, NV FILLIPPO (unverified),
  CHATEAU LES VERENES vs LES VERGES (one is wrong), MALBOUROUGH, RIEBBEKSREVIER, DOGC, LOGANN'S (unverified),
  BLENDED SCOTH, BIGMAC's "SHERRY CASK" vs the list's "SHERRY OAK".
- **`price_notes`**: three bundles' struck-through "was" prices are not the sum of the list prices
  (Rockstar Breakfast 7,180 vs 7,380; Shogun's Potion 16,980 vs 17,980; BigMac 20,480 vs 21,480). Campari and
  Aperol print one price (glass). Wines print bottle prices only; no wine by the glass. No cocktail page.
- **Game data** `game/data/menu.json`, generated by `python tools/menu_build.py` (never edit by hand): 155 items
  (121 drinks, 34 kitchen; the waterfall splits into pork and beef; the house shooter is an item; the ice bucket is
  left out). Fields: `id` (slug of the printed name, stable), `name_en` (title case; clear misprints corrected,
  unverified ones kept), `name_th` + `th_draft`, `name_printed`, `category`, `section`, `group`, `effect`, `price`
  (THB; per glass for spirits, per bottle for wine), `price_unit`, `price_bottle`, `star`, `chilli`, `abv`,
  `region`, `notes`, `pourable`, `pour_style`. Also `effects`, `try_order`, `pour_featured`, `pour_merry`,
  `pour_colors`, `sections`, `bundles`.
- **Thai** is a machine draft for Black Cabin staff (`th_draft: true`): food and generic drinks translated; wine and
  spirit brand names kept in Latin script, as Bangkok bar menus print them. The two Thai names printed on the menu
  (ยำไข่มดแดง, ยำไข่เป็ดดาว) are used as printed (`th_draft: false`).
- **Strings** `game/i18n/strings_menu.csv` (EN + TH, 43 keys), read at runtime by `BarMenu.t()` (no project.godot
  change).
- **Code** `game/scripts/menu/bar_menu.gd` (static loader, names by locale, effects, prices, Try order, Pour picks
  and colours), `game/scripts/menu/menu_list.gd` (the Drinks / Kitchen sheet), `game/scripts/minigames/pour_perfect.gd`
  (takes `config.drink`: "Pouring: <drink>" above the glass, layer colours by pour style; the lab still pours plain).
- **Tests** `game/tests/test_menu.gd` (~2,360 checks); capture run `game/tests/capture_menu.gd`.
