## Gotchas
- The overlay runs on its own clock (`step(dt)` from `_process`); tests set `auto_step = false` and call `step`.
  Captures that drive `advance(dt)` by hand must also step the overlay, or pops pile up (see the capture script).
- In `SceneTree._initialize` the root is not in the tree yet, so `_ready` / `_enter_tree` of nodes added there do
  not run; the overlay finds its shake target lazily in `step`.
