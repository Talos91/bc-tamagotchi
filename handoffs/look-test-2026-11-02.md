## What exists
- `game/tests/capture_look_test.gd`: runtime look test. It applies a variant (wall, ceiling and floor materials, the
  42 lights' colours and energies, the environment) to `main.tscn` as built, then captures the game's views with the
  HUD on. Windowed only. Variants A today, B black + red, C burgundy + red, D as in the venue (Daniele's chat note:
  grey-top north wall with a neon line, all other walls blackish, cement floor). Add a variant by adding a dict to
  `VARIANTS`; add a view to `VIEWS`.
- `game/tools/look_test_compose.py`: strips (A | B | C | D) and photo sheets into `docs/art/screens/look-test/`.

## How it works / gotchas
- Material lookup is by `resource_name` (`main_wall`, `Upper Brick`, `Floor`, kit `CabinDark`, …). Light lookup is by
  node name (`LIGHT_CATS`). After a venue rebuild, check the first printed line (42 lights, 17 materials, 2 floor
  materials, 5 north wall pieces).
- For textured materials (bond floor/brick), the variant colour multiplies the texture.
- The camera rig's sinking stubs share the wall's `material_override`. The test sets the override on wall and stub,
  found by name `WallNorth*` under `MainRoom`.
- Lesson: wall albedo hardly shows in the game cameras. Light, floor and contrast decide the look. A red ambient or
  strong red lamps make the player vanish (red on green is black). Keep the ambient near neutral and strong, and keep
  the walls dark by albedo.
- Run with `APPDATA` pointed at an empty folder. The shader-cache `err != OK` lines that appear then are harmless.
