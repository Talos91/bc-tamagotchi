# iPhone test script (for Daniele's friend; perf-iphone 2026-11-20)

1. On the iPhone, in Safari, open **https://blackcabin.app/?perf=1** on Wi-Fi (a small fps box shows top left). Note roughly how long until "Tap to enter" shows.
2. Tap to enter, make or skip the character, walk in through the door and stand in the long corridor for 10 seconds.
3. Tap the area buttons Floor, Stage, Bar and Lounge in turn; stay about 10 seconds on each.
4. Play one minigame from the bar (any), to the end.
5. Tap **Debug** (bottom left) > **Performance** > **Copy perf report**.
6. Paste the report into a message to Daniele, plus: the iPhone model, the iOS version, and whether it felt smooth, stuttered or showed a red "lost its graphics" banner (a screenshot of the banner if it did).
7. If the page reloads or goes blank on its own, say at which step.
8. Then once more from the home-screen icon (Share > Add to Home Screen) and say if that felt any different.
