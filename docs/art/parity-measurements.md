# Parity measurements: renders 05 and 06

Measured 2026-10-28 from `direction/05-main-room-iso-from-entrance.webp` (1672 x 941, 16:9) and
`direction/06-main-room-iso-from-bar.webp` (1446 x 1087, 4:3), the quality bar (DECISIONS 2026-10-28).
The renders have no scale bar, so sizes are derived by chaining known real objects: a chair seat is 0.45 m,
a bar-stool seat 0.75 m, a table top 0.75 m, and the floor tiles (counted under furniture) are 0.25 m.
1 voxel = 5 cm (art bible section 2). Where 05 and 06 disagree (they are separate AI renders), 05 wins and
the difference is noted. Everything in the kit, the venue generator and the characters follows this table.
