**What coins are:** the tab you run at your local. Earned by going out, spent
on choice (which drink, which shirt, a room upstairs) and, later, a small
thank-you at the real bar. Never the reason to visit: needs are. **No decor:**
coins never build or change the club; the room is authored.

**Earn** (per Bangkok day/week; caps 60 a day, 250 a week, because coins reach the real bar)

| Source | Coins |
|---|---|
| First visit of the day | 10 |
| Minigame result good / great / perfect | 2 / 3 / 5 (first 4 per visit, then 1); a miss earns nothing |
| New memory | 2 (3 per visit) |
| Heart gained with a regular | 10 |
| Applause during a set | 1 (2 sets per visit) |
| Welcome Back | 10 |
| Week Recap (Monday) | 5 per day visited, max 25 |
| Starting wallet | 20, once |

**Spend**

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **First drink of every visit, water, Pour Perfect** | Free, always |
| Later drinks, banded from the real menu's THB price per glass or serve: up to 250 THB / 251–500 / over 500 | 4 / 6 / 9 (25 / 55 / 23 of the 103 by-the-glass drinks). Bottles and bundles: shown with dimmed THB, not sold for coins. Alcohol is never cheaper than a soft drink |
| Tip jar at the stage (Crowd Vibe +10, your request counts double) | 3 |
| Buy a round at a table | 3 per person |
| Wardrobe at the **merch shop in the lounge** (cosmetic merch for the character): small / mid / performer tee / statement | 30 / 60 / 80 / 120 |
| Memory Wall frames (plain free) | 20–50 |
| **The Flat** (rented room upstairs, `06-stakes` §9): rent every Monday Bangkok time, paused Out of Town or as a ghost; 7 days' grace, then moved out with things to storage; re-rent free of the move-in within 30 days | 100 to move in, then **20 a week** |

**A week, roughly** (GDD sample visits)

| Player | Earns | Drinks, tips, a round | Flat rent | Left to save |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 visit a week | ~35 | ~10 | 20 | ~5 |
| 3 visits a week | ~115 | ~35 | 20 | ~60 |
| 7 visits a week | ~240 (near the cap) | ~80 | 20 | ~140 |

**Sinks** (coins have real value, so they must drain): paid drinks and tips
(small, every visit), **Flat rent (the steady one)**, wardrobe and frames
(goals), real-world rewards (rare, capped), and **death**: a deleted
character's wallet is gone (`06-stakes` §6). No sink ever buys relief from a
need: every meter can be filled to 100 with zero coins.

Claimed in the app, shown as a rotating QR + 4-letter code, verified by staff
in seconds, one per real visit. A real check-in at the bar is what keeps the
character alive (the 21-day clock) and revives a ghost (`06-stakes` §3, §5.2).

**Never:** coins sold for real money; coins traded between players; coins
lost **while the character lives** (no expiry, no negative balance, no fines,
annoyances never cost coins);
timers, "only N left" or streak nudges; anything that looks like baht.
