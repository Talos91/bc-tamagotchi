# Black Cabin Tamagotchi — Original Brief

Source: Daniele's game-dev pipeline session, 2026-09-22 (Talos Vault, game-dev hub).

> A cozy voxel social game based on the real Bangkok live-music bar.
> Players create a character who needs their weekly dose of music,
> friendship, drinks, and nightlife, then visit a persistent digital
> recreation of the venue populated by other players' characters.
> Inside, they can meet regulars, attend performances, customize their
> appearance, collect memories, and play short activities inspired by live
> music and bar culture. The fixed venue acts as the game's living
> foundation, with new performers, events, decorations, characters, and
> minigames added over time.

## The real place

Black Cabin is a live-music bar in Bangkok. Reference photos of the real
venue live in `docs/reference/venue-photos/` (12 photos, 2026-10-27):

- Main room: concrete floor, dark walls, floor-to-ceiling red velvet
  curtains, round wooden tables with candle lamps, low leather stools,
  backlit bar with amber-lit bottle shelves, a mirror disco ball, stage
  area under red wash lighting with drum kit, amps, mic stands.
- Second space / lounge: raw concrete beams painted black, red neon-lit
  "BLACK CABIN" sign over a plant wall, long communal table, vintage CRT
  TV, a pinball-style cabinet, globe lamps, a staircase up.
- Palette: near-black, deep red, amber/warm candlelight, red neon.

## Shared pipeline (from the game-dev hub)

High concept → GDD (pillars, core loop, systems) → art bible → character /
cast bibles → UI style guide + wireframes → asset lists. Visual sheets
follow the art bible, never precede it. Every game gets its own style
anchor before any other sheet.

## Name note

The working name here is "Black Cabin Tamagotchi": the "Tamagotchi" angle
is the character's recurring needs (music, friends, drinks, nightlife)
that decay over the week and are refilled by visiting the venue.
